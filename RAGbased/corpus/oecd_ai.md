# OECD AI Principles (2019, updated 2024) — paraphrase

Source: OECD, "Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence", OECD/LEGAL/0449. Paraphrased and condensed.

## Inclusive growth, sustainable development, well-being
AI should benefit people and the planet by driving inclusive growth, sustainable development, and well-being. Deployments that harm vulnerable groups or the environment fall short of this standard.

## Human rights and democratic values
AI systems should be designed in a way that respects the rule of law, human rights, democratic values, and diversity. They should include appropriate safeguards — for example human review — to allow for a fair and just society.

## Transparency and explainability
There should be transparency and responsible disclosure around AI systems so that people understand AI-based outcomes and can challenge them when they are wrong or harmful.

## Robustness, security and safety
AI systems must function in a robust, secure, and safe way throughout their lifecycles. Potential risks should be continually assessed and managed.

## Accountability
Organisations and individuals developing, deploying, or operating AI systems should be held accountable for their proper functioning in line with these principles. "The model did it" is not a valid excuse.

## Investing in trustworthy AI
Governments should invest in AI research and development that focuses on trustworthy AI and addresses challenging technical issues.

## Fostering a digital ecosystem for AI
Governments should foster digital ecosystems for trustworthy AI, including digital infrastructure and mechanisms to share data and knowledge responsibly.

## Shaping an enabling policy environment
An agile policy environment is needed that supports a transition from research to deployment while protecting people and society.

## Building human capacity and preparing for labour-market transformation
Governments should empower people with the skills to engage with AI, and support workers through transition.

## International cooperation
Governments should cooperate across borders and sectors to share information, develop standards, and promote stewardship of AI.
